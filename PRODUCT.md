# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People building a personal family-health and prevention profile. They use the app to record family history, answer lifestyle questions, understand educational prevention priorities, and find practical local wellness actions.

## Product Purpose

Family Health helps users turn family-history and lifestyle inputs into clear, educational prevention guidance. Success means users understand what patterns may be worth discussing with a healthcare professional and leave with simple next actions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, practical. The product should feel like a polished consumer health tool: supportive and clear, without sounding clinical, alarming, or diagnostic.

## Anti-references

Avoid school-project dashboards, cluttered medical reports, scare-based risk language, fake precision, decorative health gimmicks, and resource lists that feel generic or unrelated to the user's actual profile.

## Design Principles

- Make the next healthy action obvious.
- Explain recommendations with evidence from the user's reported profile.
- Keep medical language educational, transparent, and non-diagnostic.
- Prefer compact, scannable cards over long reports.
- Preserve privacy by keeping sensitive family-health data local.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high-contrast text, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible cards and controls, readable font sizes, and do not rely on color alone to communicate status or priority.
