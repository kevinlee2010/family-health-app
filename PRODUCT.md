# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People building a private family-health profile who want to understand patterns in family history, lifestyle habits, and nearby preventive-health opportunities before discussing them with a healthcare professional.

## Product Purpose

The app helps users organize family health history and lifestyle information into educational prevention insights, daily habits, and local health opportunities. Success means the user feels informed, not diagnosed, and can take practical next steps.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, encouraging. The interface should feel like a polished consumer health dashboard: simple enough for everyday users, careful enough for sensitive health information.

## Anti-references

Avoid medical-alarm language, clinical diagnosis framing, cluttered report pages, generic event listings, and school-project visual treatment.

## Design Principles

- Explain without diagnosing.
- Make the next healthy action obvious.
- Keep sensitive data local and reassure users through clear copy.
- Prefer concise, scannable cards over long reports.
- Make local resources feel directly tied to the user’s actual profile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable type sizes, strong contrast, visible focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, and language that remains encouraging for users with incomplete or concerning health histories.
